Overview

Mumbai is filled with cultural spaces, local businesses, old cafés, theatres and communities that carry stories of the city, but many of them remain difficult to discover.

UNSEEN is a mobile app designed to help people discover these lesser-known places through curated recommendations, local stories, maps and community-driven exploration.

THE PROBLEM

Most discovery platforms make popular places easy to find, but leave lesser-known cultural spaces scattered across social media, blogs, maps and word-of-mouth recommendations.

This makes discovering authentic local experiences more difficult — while increasing visibility can also risk changing the character of the communities and places being discovered.

How might we…

make Mumbai's hidden cultural experiences easier to discover while keeping their stories, people and authenticity at the centre?

COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

What we found ?

Existing platforms are effective at helping people find places, but they rarely combine location, cultural context, local stories and community perspectives in one experience.

This became the foundation for UNSEEN.

PRIMARY RESEARCH

We conducted an online survey targeting both locals and tourists who have explored Mumbai. A total of 35+ respondents participated, providing insights into how people currently discover cultural spots and what challenges they face.

Key Findings

  • People often discover unfamiliar places through recommendations and word of mouth.
  • There is interest in exploring lesser-known places, but people often don't know where to find them.
  • People value authentic experiences over places that are simply popular or highly rated.
  • Local places can be shared more widely, but respect and authenticity matter.
