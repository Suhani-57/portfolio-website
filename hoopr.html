Listening To...

An Exploration Of Music Through Image, Typography, Colour And Visual Storytelling.

Music Doesn't Just Sound A Certain Way — It Feels A Certain Way.

During my internship at Hoopr, I explored how those feelings could be translated into visual worlds. Each song became a starting point for a different visual direction, allowing me to experiment with imagery, typography, colour, composition and art direction.

Rather than creating one fixed visual style, I approached every track as its own story.

What I made ?

I created a collection of digital music artworks spanning romantic tracks, Indian and folk-inspired music, dreamy compositions, energetic releases and travel-inspired sounds.

My role involved developing visual concepts, exploring imagery and references, composing layouts, experimenting with typography and creating the final digital artworks.

The process was less about following one aesthetic and more about asking:

What should this song look like?

That question became the guiding principle across different client projects I worked on over a period of three months.
