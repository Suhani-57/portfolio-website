About Me

I’m naturally curious, which means I like seeing where an idea can go before deciding what it should be. I’ll ask questions, try different things, change things around.

I like exploring design in all the ways it can exist; from branding and illustration to UI/UX, digital experiences and everything in between. I enjoy taking an idea that’s still a little messy and figuring out how to give it a look, a story and a personality of its own.

I think that’s what makes designing fun for me. There’s rarely just one right answer, and there’s always another possibility worth exploring.
